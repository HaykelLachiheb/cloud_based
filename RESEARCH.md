# Smart Waste Management Platform: Research & Problem Justification

## Problem Overview
Urban areas worldwide face significant challenges in waste management, including overflowing bins, inefficient collection routes, and low recycling rates. These issues contribute to environmental pollution, increased operational costs, and public dissatisfaction.

## Literature & Industry Review
- According to the World Bank, global waste is expected to grow by 70% by 2050 if no action is taken (Kaza et al., 2018).
- Smart waste management systems using IoT and cloud technologies have shown to reduce collection costs by up to 30% and improve recycling rates (Patel et al., 2021, IEEE Access).
- Cities like Barcelona and Seoul have implemented smart bin monitoring, resulting in cleaner streets and optimized logistics (Smart Cities Dive, 2022).

## Proposed Solution
A cloud-based platform that enables:
- Citizens to report full/overflowing bins via a web/mobile interface.
- City officials to monitor bin status, optimize collection routes, and analyze waste trends.
- Real-time dashboard for bin status, collection schedules, and recycling stats.

## Impact
- Reduces operational costs and environmental impact.
- Improves citizen engagement and satisfaction.
- Supports data-driven policy and sustainability goals.
